01 — About

Turning data into audit and business insight.

This portfolio brings together interactive learning and project-based work in financial auditing, audit analytics, data analysis, risk assessment, and fraud detection. The projects are designed to explore how structured data, analytical methods, and visual insights can support better decision-making and operational accuracy.

02 — Areas of Focus

Core capabilities

Financial Auditing Audit fundamentals, audit procedures, financial data review
Data Analytics Data analysis, correlation, classification, analytical interpretation
Risk Analysis Risk profiling, segmentation, risk-based auditing
Fraud Detection Fraud indicators, transaction analysis, predictive evaluation
